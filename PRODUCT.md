## Platform

web

## Users

This public website is for fleet and logistics operators evaluating DevNullX,
and for investors or strategic partners evaluating the wider relay-logistics
network. The operating product also serves drivers, small fleet owners, fleet
administrators, and eventually shippers.

## Product Purpose

DevNullX reduces uncertainty, safety risk, fragmented records, and avoidable
cost in Indian road logistics.

Aghrahan is the edge-first driver-safety and fleet-operations platform. An
in-vehicle Axon device observes safety events, video, location, and vehicle
telemetry; the cloud, operator console, and driver application turn that
evidence into operational awareness.

Relay logistics is the equal second pillar. Long-haul trips become managed legs
with driver handoffs, relay points, trip records, and settlement workflows.
Safety and fleet visibility are the practical entry point; the relay network is
the larger operating model they enable.

## Positioning

DevNullX is not only a telematics dashboard or a standalone driver-monitoring
camera. Its distinct mechanism joins:

1. edge safety intelligence inside the truck,
2. live and event-based operational evidence in the cloud,
3. fleet and driver workflows across web and mobile, and
4. a relay-handoff marketplace built around the same vehicle and driver
   context.

## Confirmed Capability

- On-edge driver monitoring for drowsiness, distraction, fatigue-related
  states, and camera obstruction or tampering.
- Live camera streaming and event-linked video clips.
- GPS and CAN/OBD telemetry paths, with some vehicle-bus validation pending.
- A web fleet-operations console and Flutter driver application.
- Device provisioning, health monitoring, over-the-air updates, and remote
  operational tooling.
- Relay legs, trips, wallets, relay points, and handoff workflows.
- An end-to-end development stack and an Axon development path with two cameras
  streaming on AGR-006.

## Durable Constraints

- The system is live in a development environment; production is a roadmap
  item.
- The active truck configuration uses two cameras.
- Customer outcomes, safety-reduction results, savings figures, and
  network-scale results are not available as public proof.
- Illustrative dashboard data, routes, events, and relay operations must be
  labeled as product demonstrations.
- Safety is introduced first and relay logistics follows, but both are equal
  pillars.
- The public website has no contact email or submitting form yet.

## Product Principles

1. Evidence before promise.
2. Edge reliability first.
3. One operational story from cab to control room to relay.
4. Built for Indian HCV logistics.
5. Ambition without fabrication.
6. WCAG 2.2 AA as the accessibility baseline, including reduced motion.
